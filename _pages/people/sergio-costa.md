## About

Sérgio Souza Costa is an Associate Professor at the Federal University of Maranhão (UFMA), where he leads the LambdaGeo research group and is a permanent faculty member of the Graduate Program in Environmental Science and Technology (PPGC&TAmb). His research develops computational methods for understanding environmental change: spatially explicit models of land use and land cover change, deforestation and coastal impacts in the Brazilian Amazon and Cerrado, and the open, reproducible software and data infrastructure these models depend on.

He holds MSc and PhD degrees in Applied Computing from the National Institute for Space Research (INPE), where his doctoral work addressed regional-scale agent-based modelling of land change in Amazonian frontier areas and he contributed to the TerraME/LuccME modelling platforms.

## Research interests

- Land use and land cover change (LUCC) modelling
- Dynamic spatial modelling: cellular automata and agent-based models
- Environmental impacts: deforestation, sea-level rise, coastal ecosystems
- Reproducible and FAIR scientific software
- Linked Data and semantic geoinformatics
- Functional programming for scientific modelling

## Current projects

- **[DisSModel](/software/dissmodel/)** — Python framework for discrete spatial simulation and land-change modelling, successor to INPE's TerraME/LuccME ([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23246106](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23246106)).
- **Land change in Maranhão** — deforestation in the Maranhão Cerrado and land-cover classification accuracy in the São Luís region, with open code for each study.
- **[QGISSPARQL](/software/qgissparql/)** — integration of Linked Data and GIS ([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23102044](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23102044)).
- **Open textbooks** — geoprocessing, remote sensing and geospatial modelling with Python ([lambdageo-edu](https://lambdageo-edu.github.io/)).
